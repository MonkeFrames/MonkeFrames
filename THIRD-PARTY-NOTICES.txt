Volumetric clouds (Graphics > Clouds)
-------------------------------------
The cloud ray marching, noise shaping, lighting, multi-scattering, powder effect, temporal
denoise, bilateral upscale and cloud shadow code in the embedded "monkeframes_gfx" shader
bundle is adapted from UnityVolumetricCloudsURP by jiaozi158
(https://github.com/jiaozi158/UnityVolumetricCloudsURP), a URP port of Unity HDRP's
volumetric clouds, used under the MIT License below. MonkeFrames changes: local fly-through
cloud volume against the scene depth, weather-map coverage, sunlight through a physical
atmosphere, sky light from the atmosphere and aerial perspective.

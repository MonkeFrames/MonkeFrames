The "Casting style" first person (Other Cameras > First Person) follows CastingShouldBeFree's
FirstPersonModeHandler / CameraHandler: the camera rides inside the watched gorilla's head mesh,
0.15 m up, with the head's rotation and turn-only smoothing, and their hat and face cosmetics are
hidden. MonkeFrames hides them from its own camera only (VR is unchanged).
